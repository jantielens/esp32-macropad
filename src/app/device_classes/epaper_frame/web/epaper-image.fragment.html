Advanced: Timer Wake Protection

Limits energy spent on scheduled refreshes when WiFi, the image service, or MQTT is slow or unavailable. When a limit is reached, the device keeps the image already on screen and returns to deep sleep. Manual button refreshes and cold boots are not limited. An in-progress panel refresh is never interrupted.

Recalculated when the offline image count or network timings change. Includes the first image, every offline image, and 10 seconds for panel work and NTP.
