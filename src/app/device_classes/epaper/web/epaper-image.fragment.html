📰 Image & Schedule

Where the dashboard image is fetched from and how often the device wakes to refresh it. The device deep-sleeps between refreshes to save battery.

Image source
PNG, JPG, or BMP. Sized for your panel (720×1280 for Inkplate 5 V2). The device also fetches <url>.crc32 as a change-detection sidecar so unchanged images skip the slow refresh.
Refresh schedule
The device deep-sleeps between refreshes to save battery. 900 = 15 minutes. Set to 0 for button-only wake (no timer).
After repeated WiFi failures the sleep interval doubles up to this ceiling. 3600 = 1 hour.
Frontlight
Only used on button wakes — periodic refreshes do not light the panel.
