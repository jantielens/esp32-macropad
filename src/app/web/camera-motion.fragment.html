Motion sensing

Detect camera motion locally and expose it as motion-derived presence.

Analyzes camera frames locally while enabled, including when the display sleeps. It does not create JPEG or preview frames when no other camera client is active.
Motion analyzes every Nth captured frame. At the current camera capture rate, this is approximately 2 FPS.
Higher sensitivity detects smaller changes but can react to shadows and lighting changes.
Presence stays on for this period after the last detected motion.
Verify Camera Presence in the Sensor Data fragment or on the camera_presence/state MQTT topic.
