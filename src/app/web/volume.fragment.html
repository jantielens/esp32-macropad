Set the device audio output level and amplifier behavior.

Volume
Device audio volume (0–100%). Applies to alerts, Music, and button feedback.
Amplifier

Some amplifiers hiss while powered on, even when no sound is playing. Idle muting keeps them quiet between sounds, at the cost of a short wake-up delay.

On (default): mute after playback finishes and buffered audio drains. Off: keep the amplifier enabled between sounds for faster button feedback; idle hiss may return. This does not disable audio playback.
Default: 120 ms. Waits after enabling the amplifier before sending audio so the beginning of a sound is not clipped. Lower values reduce latency but may cut off short tones; 0 removes the wait. Range: 0-1000 ms.
