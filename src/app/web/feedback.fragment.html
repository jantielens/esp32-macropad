Button Feedback

Configure the tone patterns played after button gestures.

Tone patterns use freq:duration pairs (Hz:ms). Separate tones with spaces. A bare number is a silent gap.
Examples: 800:80 (single tap), 600:40 40 600:40 (double chirp).
