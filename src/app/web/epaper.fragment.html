📰 E-Paper

The device wakes on a schedule, downloads an image, renders it on the e-paper panel, and goes back to sleep. Hold the wake button during boot to enter Config Mode (this portal).

Image source
PNG, JPG, or BMP. Sized for your panel (720×1280 for Inkplate 5 V2). The device also fetches <url>.crc32 as a change-detection sidecar so unchanged images skip the slow refresh.
Refresh schedule
The device deep-sleeps between refreshes to save battery. 900 = 15 minutes.
After repeated WiFi failures the sleep interval doubles up to this ceiling. 3600 = 1 hour.
