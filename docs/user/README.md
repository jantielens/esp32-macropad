Devices are configured through a local web portal, with controls that match the
installed firmware. Use the shared guides for settings and everyday tasks,
and the hardware reference for your board's connections and recovery controls.

## Start here

* [Install firmware, join Wi-Fi, and make your first button](getting-started.md)
* [Find your board, connections, and recovery procedure](hardware.md)
* [Open the portal and understand Save and Reboot](portal.md)

## Configure a device

* [Change Wi-Fi, hostname, IP settings, or portal login](device/network-and-access.md)
* [Set brightness, rotation, and idle/sleep behavior](device/display-and-sleep.md)
* [Set the device timezone and check synchronization](device/time.md)
* [Set an alarm](device/alarms.md) or [build a countdown/stopwatch](device/timers.md)
* [Configure boot, swipe, physical-button, or MQTT-triggered actions](device/triggers.md)
* [Browse storage](device/storage.md), [back up data](device/backup-and-restore.md),
  or [update and recover firmware](device/updates-and-recovery.md)
* [Check health, logs, crashes, and screen preview](device/diagnostics.md)

The [device task index](device/README.md) groups these settings.
The portal shows only features included in the installed firmware.
